Curious BBA student sets sights on business, finance and marketing

By Surya Gupta, the subject of this story

Hi, I’m Surya. I like finding out how companies really work, how finance keeps them going and how marketing brings customers in.

I believe good ideas start with listening. So I stay curious, ask questions and try things out. Whether I’m in a team or on my own, I break big problems into small steps, look at them from different angles, and keep improving until they work.

I enjoy learning from different points of view and turning ideas into action. Outside class, you’ll find me cycling, watching movies or playing badminton.
